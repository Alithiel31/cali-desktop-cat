## [0.5.0] - 2026-10-23

### Added

- **Jazz speaks up**: he has his own bubble (click-through) with barks ("Wouf !", "Ouaf !"...) and, more rarely, the
  "Toller scream" ("Aouaouaouaou !"). He barks now and then while crossing the screen, calls Cali with a scream when he
  spots her, falls silent on arriving next to her so she can answer, lets out a small "Ouaf ♥" during the get-together
  and sighs when he lies down against her. He follows the bubbles setting, renamed "Miauler et aboyer de temps en
  temps" ("Meow and bark now and then"); his lines use their own random numbers (`voice`).
- **Cali carries the mouse**: after a successful pounce on the cursor, she holds it under her paws and takes it wherever
  she goes (walking, jumping, falling) until you move the mouse again. She does not fall asleep on it, lets go if you
  pick her up or a focus session starts, and does not hunt again for 25 s. Setting "Emporter la souris après le bond"
  ("Carry the mouse after the pounce"; on by default, no effect if "Chasser la souris" is off).
- **Jazz, Cali's big brother**: a pixel-art Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever (`desktopcat/dog.py` and
  `dog_sprite.py`) pays Cali a visit from time to time (first visit 2 to 5 min after launch, then every 6 to 15 min).
  He crosses the screen, comes to greet her (she turns to him, hearts and a bubble, then often follows him for a few
  seconds when he leaves) or, if she is napping, lies down against her without waking her. Transparent window with no
  mouse input, random numbers kept separate from the cat's; never during a focus session, nor while the cat is hidden or
  asleep. "Jazz rend visite à Cali" ("Jazz visits Cali") setting (on by default) and a test button in the Settings;
  `tools/preview_jazz.py` renders a preview of his drawing.
- **English documentation**: `README.en.md`, `tools/README.en.md` and `docs/en/` (one version of every document in
  `docs/`, plus `CONTRIBUTING`, `SECURITY`, `SUPPORT`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT` and `CHANGELOG`). Every French document links
  to its English version, and vice versa.

### Changed

- **Distribution**: the SignPath Foundation application was rejected; the chosen route is the Microsoft Store.
  Documentation (README, distribution, roadmap, security, handover guide) updated; the README's "Code signing policy"
  section and the commented-out SignPath block of `release.yml` removed.
- CI: `actions/download-artifact` moves to v8, in line with `upload-artifact` v7.
- **Reproducible builds**: `requirements-build.txt` pins PySide6 (6.12.0) and PyInstaller (6.22.3) to build the exe;
  `release.yml` uses it instead of an unversioned `pip install pyinstaller`.
- **ruff linter**: `pyproject.toml` (120 columns, rules E, F, W, I, B, UP), blocking `lint` job in `ci.yml`,
  `requirements-dev.txt`. Code brought into line: sorted imports, `l` variables renamed, assigned lambdas replaced by `def`,
  `zip(strict=…)`, long lines wrapped; no behaviour change. No automatic formatter.
- **Versioned `DesktopCat.spec`**: the build options (icon, metadata, no console, UPX disabled) live in the `.spec`, used by
  the local build and by `release.yml`. `*.spec` leaves `.gitignore`.
- Repository tidy-up: historical prototypes moved from `tools/spikes/` to `archive/spikes/`; global `*.png` rule removed
  from `.gitignore` (only `tools/out/` stays ignored), so screenshots and images can be versioned.
- Documentation brought up to date for 0.4.1: architecture (slowed loop, modules, test table), roadmap (figures, levels,
  `pet.py`), distribution (examples, signing), troubleshooting (overlays, Ctrl+Alt+C, `StartupApproved`), handover
  guide, contributing, security (global hotkey, registry).

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-19

First published Release (0.3.0 was never tagged: its content is included here).

### Added

- **Feeding the cat**: mood from 0 to 100, meals of 5 kibble (panel, right-click, tray icon). When full, it brings
  gifts 25 % faster; when starving, it begs. Petting helps a little.
- **Seasonal hat** "Citrouille" (Pumpkin), from 25 October to 2 November.
- **Mouse hunting**: it watches the mouse that has just stopped near it, then pounces (can be turned off).
- **Pomodoro break** after every successful session (5 min, adjustable), ☕ timer in the bubble, "Passer la pause"
  ("Skip the break") button.
- **Ctrl+Alt+C shortcut** to show or hide the cat (can be turned off).

- "Noter les applications utilisées" ("Record the apps used") setting to turn off active-executable tracking.
- "Effacer mes statistiques" ("Erase my statistics") button (with confirmation): empties the history, readable backups
  included.

- Project documentation: `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `SUPPORT.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, this `CHANGELOG.md`,
  issue and PR templates, `CODEOWNERS`, Dependabot, and in `docs/`: `DEVELOPPEMENT.md`, `DISTRIBUTION.md`,
  `GUIDE-DE-REPRISE.md`, `ACCESSIBILITE.md` (draft), `VEILLE-DEPENDANCES.md`.

### Changed

- Battery: no more `timeBeginPeriod(1)` (system timer forced to 1 ms permanently); the cat's loop goes from ~60 to 10
  iterations per second when it is hidden or asleep because the user is away.
- Save file in v2 format: `gift_pending` (yes/no) becomes `gifts_pending` (number of waiting gifts). v1 saves are
  migrated automatically (a `save.json.v1.bak` copy is kept).
- A waiting gift lets the current bubble finish ("Te revoilà !", the hat just opened) before appearing.

### Fixed

- The gift from a focus session is no longer lost when an ordinary gift was already waiting: they add up.
- Daily save copy also when the app runs for several days without restarting (PC put to sleep).
- Save forced to disk (`fsync`) before the replacement: a power cut no longer leaves an empty file. The replacement is
  retried if an antivirus locks the file for a moment.
- The cat no longer perches on invisible windows: click-through transparent overlays (Steam, NVIDIA, Discord), windows
  that cannot be activated, stacked zero-opacity windows.
- Window titles are read without sending them a message (`InternalGetWindowText`): a window that is "not responding"
  can no longer freeze the cat.
- Launch with Windows: the registry is the source of truth. The checkbox follows a disabling done in Task Manager
  (`StartupApproved`), re-ticking it really re-enables startup, and the exe's path is rewritten at launch if it has
  changed (exe moved or updated).
- `tools/run_test_instance.bat` uses the project's `.venv` Python first.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-18

First complete version, never tagged or published: all its content is part of 0.4.0.

### Added

- Pixel-art cat (tortoiseshell coat) that wanders around the Windows desktop, perches on window edges, falls when they
  disappear, jumps from one window to another and falls asleep after a period of inactivity.
- Multi-screen: one floor per screen, walking across adjacent screens, jumping or falling between the others, live
  plugging and unplugging, full screen that only hides the cat on its own screen.
- Gifts and hats: 6 rarities, weighted draw, resale of duplicates for kibble.
- Focus sessions (1 to 180 min), reward conditioned on duration (≥ 10 min) and presence (≥ 50 %), resumed after an app
  restart.
- Control panel: wardrobe, focus, statistics (7 days + apps of the day), settings.
- Tray icon, meow bubbles, launch with Windows (optional).
- Error log (`desktopcat.log`, 1 MB rotation) reachable from the tray icon's menu.
- Robust save file: atomic write, timestamped copies of an unreadable file (3 kept), daily copy (7 kept) with automatic
  restore and notification, format version and migrations.
- Machine-independent tests (fake screens, fake mouse, fake desktop) and a guard against real screens.
- GitHub Actions CI (Windows and Ubuntu 24.04, Python 3.12 and 3.13); CD: `DesktopCat.exe` build and Release
  publication with SHA256 hash on a `v*` tag.
- Documentation: README, architecture, game rules, troubleshooting, roadmap, development tools.

### Changed

- The meow bubble's tail now points at the cat's head.
- The time counted by the game is the time actually elapsed, capped at 5 s per iteration.

### Fixed

- The instance lock left by a crash is taken over after 30 s.
- A PC sleep no longer skews statistics or gift progress.
- Unreadable bubble in Windows dark theme: drawn background, text forced dark, size tied to the cat's scale.
- While working, the cat stays fully visible (the computer used to go off-screen).
- The cat no longer teleports when landing on a window moved during the jump.
- Particles ("z", hearts) offset on the second screen: one effects window per screen.
- CI: Python 3.10 removed (crashes with PySide6 6.12), Ubuntu 24.04 pinned.
