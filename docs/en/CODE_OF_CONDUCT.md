# Code of Conduct

[Français](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · English

## Our pledge

In the interest of keeping an open and welcoming space, people who take part in this project — through an issue, a pull
request, a discussion or any other exchange related to the repository — pledge to make it a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of experience, identity or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Show respect for viewpoints and experiences different from your own.
- Accept constructive criticism openly.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Be patient — this project is maintained by one person, in their spare time.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexual language or imagery, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, public or private.
- Publishing a third party's private information (address, email...) without their explicit consent.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to
[contact@alithiel31.dev](mailto:contact@alithiel31.dev). Every complaint will be reviewed and will receive a response
deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The person reporting an incident can expect their report to be
handled confidentially.

The project maintainer has the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for
behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) as well as in public when
a person represents the project or its community.
